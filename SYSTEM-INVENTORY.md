## Tooling around the components

Historical mathematical checks verified GNU Octave 11.3.0 matrix multiplication and Maxima 5.50.0 symbolic differentiation. Scientific Python, native compilation, Qt/SQLite, SQL/Excel and a limited PyTorch GPU workflow also passed scoped checks. Their exact environment bindings matter; see [ENGINEERING-KNOWLEDGE.md](ENGINEERING-KNOWLEDGE.md).

Real financial-provider connectors are not implemented or enabled in the hub. Its accounts, payments, positions and balances are synthetic. Broker-demo work belongs to the separate GENESIS-1 route. Markdown alone cannot recreate the platform, its private evidence or installed software.
